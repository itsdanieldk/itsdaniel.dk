I'm Daniel Larsen, a software engineer from Denmark. F# is my favorite language, C# is the one I write most days, and functional programming shapes how I use both.

I'm responsible for Backend Development at [Clever](https://clever.dk/), where I build the systems behind EV charging in Denmark: .NET services running on Azure. On the side I maintain [FIO](/projects/fio), a functional effect system for F# that started as my thesis at DTU in 2022. Some of what I learn ends up here as notes; the rest I share through demos.

Outside programming, I run and lift weights, explore new metal bands, practice guitar, [collect fragrances](/fragrances), and read Norse mythology and history.
